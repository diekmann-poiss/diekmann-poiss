Why
us?

We combine hands-on implementation experience with scientific rigour — two disciplines that rarely sit together in the same room.

Diekmann & Poiss grew out of years spent at the intersection of landscape architecture, building physics, and data science. We have planned, built, and monitored living wall and green roof systems across a range of building typologies — and used those projects as laboratories for the tools we now offer to clients.

Our approach is rooted in measurable outcomes: thermal comfort, plant survival rates, water efficiency, long-term maintenance cost. Every recommendation we make is one we can back with data — because in most cases we have collected that data ourselves.

We are a small practice by design. Every project is handled directly by us, not delegated. That means consistent quality, fast turnaround, and a single point of contact from first consultation to final handover.
